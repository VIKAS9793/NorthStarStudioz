Our Game Development Niches

Where tactile physics, real-world presence, and intuitive mechanics intersect

Color Passthrough Bare-Hand Tracking VRC Comfortable (0% Nausea)

Rather than building traditional enclosed VR games that induce fatigue or require bulky controllers, NorthStar Studioz specializes in high-retention Mixed Reality (MR) categories designed to be played seamlessly in everyday living spaces.

👐

Bare-Hands First Interaction

We build games that treat hands and fingers as the primary input. Utilizing Meta's Interaction SDK, players grab, pinch, rotate, and manipulate physics objects naturally without picking up controllers.

🏠

Room-Scale & Surface Meshing

Our games treat real coffee tables, desks, floors, and walls as active play surfaces. By leveraging scene understanding, virtual physics directly bounce and interact with physical architecture.

🎵

Tactile ASMR Soundscapes

Every interaction is tuned for acoustic satisfaction. We integrate micro-sampled real-world materials (wood, brass, ceramic, marble) with true 3D spatialized binaural audio for deep immersion.

🧩

Spatial Puzzles & Micro-Sandboxes

We focus on kinetic chain reactions, gravity puzzles, and open-ended sandboxes that offer immediate pick-up-and-play delight with endless player experimentation.

🛡️

Zero Motion Sickness (100% Comfort)

By rooting experiences in the player's physical environment via Passthrough, our games eliminate vestibular mismatch and boundary anxiety, achieving the highest 'Comfortable' rating.

☁️

Persistent Spatial Anchors

Apparatuses and creations stay locked where you put them. Using Meta Spatial Anchors and Cloud Storage, your workbench reappears in the exact physical room location session after session.
