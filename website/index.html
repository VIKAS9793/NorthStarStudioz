Our focus

Four principles behind everything we build.

  1. 01

    Hands first

    Your fingers are the controller. Pinch, grab, turn and snap, designed around natural hand tracking. Controllers are a fallback, not the default.

  2. 02

    Your room is the level

    Passthrough and scene understanding turn real tables, floors and walls into play surfaces. No black void, no boundary redraw. Just your space, with something new in it.

  3. 03

    Sound you can feel

    Touch lives in the ears too. We design with sampled real materials such as wood, brass and ceramic, placed in spatial audio so every click and roll lands where it happens.

  4. 04

    Comfort by design

    Stationary play with no artificial locomotion. The real room stays visible, so experiences work for first-timers and long sessions alike.

Platform

Built for Meta Quest, on open standards.

Standalone first: everything runs on the headset, with no PC required.

  • RuntimeMeta Horizon OSNative standalone builds for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S.
  • StandardOpenXR + Meta XR SDKOpen standards at the core, with Meta’s hand, passthrough and scene APIs where they make the experience better.
  • EngineUnity · URPA mobile-first render pipeline tuned for standalone headset chipsets.

The bar we hold ourselves to

  • Hold the headset’s native refresh rate, every frame.
  • Open straight into passthrough, in your room, within seconds.
  • Respect the system: boundary, tracking loss and the Meta button always behave as players expect.
