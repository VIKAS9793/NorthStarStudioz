01 · How it plays

No controllers. No black void.
No motion sickness.

  1. Reach in.

    Your hands are the controller. Pinch, grab, twist and snap things the way you would in real life. Controllers still work; you just won’t need them.

    Prompt 01 / 04

  2. Use the table.

    The game finds your real table, floor and walls and builds on top of them. Your living room stays in view the whole time.

    Prompt 02 / 04

  3. Hear it land.

    Wood clicks, brass rings, ceramic clinks. Sounds are built from real materials and placed where they happen in your room.

    Prompt 03 / 04

  4. Stay put.

    Everything happens within arm’s reach. There’s no artificial movement, so it stays comfortable for first-timers and long sessions alike.

    Prompt 04 / 04
