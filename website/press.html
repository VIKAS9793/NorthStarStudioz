About the studio

Short: NorthStar Studioz is an independent mixed reality game studio in Mumbai, India, making hand-tracked games for Meta Quest.

Long: NorthStar Studioz is an independent mixed reality game studio based in Mumbai, India, founded in October 2026 by Vikas Sahani. The studio makes games for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S that are played with bare hands on the real furniture in the player’s room, with no artificial movement and no PC required. Its first game is in development.

About the founder

Vikas Sahani works professionally in finance and has a lifelong passion for games and new technology. He started by building side projects and publishing Android apps, and founded NorthStar Studioz to make games for virtual and mixed reality on Meta Horizon OS.
