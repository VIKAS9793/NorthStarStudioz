Privacy Policy

Entity: NorthStar Studioz  |  Lead Developer: Vikas Sahani  |  Scope: NorthStar Studioz Games & Applications  |  Last Updated: October 2026

Our Privacy Pledge: NorthStar Studioz is built on user privacy and minimal data collection. Across all our games and applications, we do not store personal information on external third-party servers, we never sell your data, and we do not have access to your headset's physical camera feeds or room scans.

1. Introduction & Studio Identity

This Privacy Policy governs your use of all games, applications, and digital experiences developed and published by NorthStar Studioz, founded and led by Vikas Sahani ("we", "us", or "our"), distributed on the Meta Horizon Store and other spatial platforms.

2. Information We Access & Process

To deliver core gameplay functionality, cloud saves, and purchases, the Application interacts with the official Meta Horizon Platform SDK to process the following data:

  • Meta User ID: A platform-generated numeric identifier provided by Meta. We use this identifier solely to:
    1. Verify your game license and store entitlement upon launch (anti-piracy check).
    2. Associate your saved puzzle levels, custom sandbox creations, and game settings with Meta Cloud Storage so your progress persists across play sessions and device reinstalls.
  • Meta Username & Profile Display Name: Used exclusively on-device to personalize your in-game wrist menu and profile badge (e.g., displaying a "Welcome, [Username]" greeting).
  • In-App Purchase & Entitlement Receipts: If you purchase optional downloadable content (DLC) or add-on level packs, transaction receipts are validated via Meta's secure commerce backend to unlock purchased content locally in-game.

3. Mixed Reality, Cameras, and Spatial Data

Our Mixed Reality applications utilize Passthrough and Spatial Anchors to enable interactive digital physics and virtual apparatuses to interact with physical surfaces in your environment.

  • No Camera Feed Access: Neither NorthStar Studioz nor our applications have access to raw video feeds, images, or sensor data from your headset's physical cameras. All Passthrough video and spatial meshing are handled entirely on-device by Meta Horizon OS.
  • Local Spatial Anchors: Surface pins and tabletop anchor coordinates are stored locally or via your private Meta Cloud profile and are never transmitted to external servers.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your data. We do not operate external tracking servers, advertising networks, or third-party analytics SDKs that profile users. All network transactions (such as entitlement checks and cloud save syncing) occur directly between your headset and Meta's official secure infrastructure.

5. Children's Privacy

NorthStar Studioz games and applications are designed and intended for users aged 13 and older (or the minimum applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Because our applications store player progress using Meta Cloud Storage, your data is retained in accordance with your Meta account settings. You can delete your local save data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing local app data through the Meta Horizon OS system settings.
