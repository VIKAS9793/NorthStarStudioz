## 2. Platform Scale & Standalone vs. PC VR Market Share

### Cumulative Consumer Spend
* **$2.5 Billion - $2.9 Billion+**: Total cumulative gross consumer spend on the standalone Meta Quest / Horizon Store (milestone officially passed $2.0B at Meta Connect, now tracking between $2.8B and $3.0B).
* **Standalone Dominance:** Over **95%** of active Meta headset usage and content sales occur directly on standalone Quest hardware without a tethered PC.
* **Link PC VR Status:** Meta's legacy Rift/Link PC storefront generates negligible traffic compared to SteamVR. Developing for **Meta Horizon Store** standalone ensures direct exposure to 20M+ hardware units without requiring players to own a high-end gaming PC.

### Meta Horizon OS Open Ecosystem
* In 2024–2026, Meta opened **Meta Horizon OS** to third-party hardware manufacturers, including **ASUS Republic of Gamers (ROG)** (performance gaming spatial headsets) and **Lenovo** (productivity/spatial computing devices).
* Apps submitted to the Meta Horizon Store automatically position themselves for cross-hardware compatibility across all current Quest models and upcoming third-party Horizon OS devices.

## 4. Hardware Retention & Mixed Reality (MR) Conversion Data

### Quest 3 Retention Milestone
* **Official Metric:** Meta Quest 3 has achieved the **highest user retention rate of any headset in Meta's history**.
* **Primary Driver:** Meta's telemetry identifies **Color Passthrough and Mixed Reality experiences** as the decisive factor preventing headset abandonment.

### The "+31% Daily Active User" Lift
* In official case studies published on the Meta Developer Blog (e.g., *TRIPP* spatial experience), transitioning or expanding into Mixed Reality Passthrough generated an immediate **31% increase in 7-day average Daily Active Users (DAU)**.
* **Psychological Drivers:**
  1. **Zero Boundary Anxiety:** Players do not fear colliding with television sets, pets, or furniture.
  2. **Reduced Onboarding Friction:** Sessions launch instantly without requiring guardian redraws or room clears.
  3. **Absence of Motion Sickness:** A static physical reference frame eliminates vestibular mismatch.
