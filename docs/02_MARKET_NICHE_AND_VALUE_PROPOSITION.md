## 1. Niche Selection: Tactile Room-Scale Mixed Reality Sandbox & Puzzles

### Core Definition
**NorthStar MR** positions itself in the high-engagement **Tactile Mixed Reality (MR)** genre: a physics-driven, modular micro-sandbox where virtual mechanical contraptions, gravity conduits, and kinetic spatial puzzles are built directly onto the player's physical furniture, walls, and floor.

```

                  │          The Player's Physical Room         │
```

## 3. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

> **"Transform your living room table into a living kinetic clockwork laboratory with your bare hands."**

1. **Bare-Hands First (Zero Controller Dependency):**
   * Designed from ground zero for **Meta Interaction SDK Hand Tracking**. Controllers are supported, but pinching, rotating, placing, and snapping kinetic pieces with natural finger gestures is the primary delight.
2. **Spatial Anchors & Persistent Tabletop:**
   * Uses Meta's Spatial Anchor API. When the user sets up their contraption on their coffee table, takes off the headset, and returns hours later, the apparatus remains locked in place.
3. **ASMR & Tactile Soundscape:**
   * Every physical interaction features high-definition, micro-sampled audio (wooden clicks, magnetic snaps, glass resonances, metallic rings) paired with controller/subtle visual haptic pulses.
4. **Infinite Replayability (Curated Levels + Freeform Sandbox):**
   * Features 30 structured campaign puzzles ("Route the kinetic pulse from point A to point B using physical room constraints") PLUS a creative sandbox to design and share contraptions.

## 4. Target Player Personas

### Persona A: "The Cozy Spatial Thinker" (45% of Audience)
* **Demographics:** Age 25–50, busy professional, values mental decompression.
* **Playing Habit:** 15–30 minute evening sessions after work.
* **Pain Point:** Avoids high-speed motion sickness games (e.g., flight sims, rollercoasters). Wants relaxing, satisfying tactile problem-solving in their living room.

### Persona B: "The Tech Showcase Enthusiast" (35% of Audience)
* **Demographics:** Early adopter of Quest 3 / Quest 3S, eager to show off the headset to friends, family, and guests.
* **Playing Habit:** Uses the app as the "go-to demo" when putting a headset on a first-timer.
* **Pain Point:** Most VR games are too confusing for grandparents or children to navigate. An app that operates with intuitive hand pinches in passthrough requires zero explanation.

### Persona C: "The Kinetic Tinkerer" (20% of Audience)
* **Demographics:** Fans of *The Incredible Machine*, *Besiege*, or *Rube Goldberg* mechanics.
* **Playing Habit:** Hours spent refining intricate spatial chain reactions.

## 5. The "First 30 Seconds" Onboarding Hook

```
[0:00 - 0:05]
App launches immediately in Color Passthrough. The player sees their real room.
No black void. No disorienting loading screen.

[0:05 - 0:12]
A warm, wooden chime plays. A glowing, holographic anchor appears on the user's nearest surface (table).
A prompt gestures: "Pinch to place your Workbench."

[0:12 - 0:20]
The player pinches their fingers. A beautiful, sleek mahogany-and-brass kinetic module snaps onto their table.
A metal sphere rolls down an incline, bouncing off a brass bell with a resonant ding!

[0:20 - 0:30]
A piece floats into the player's view: "Catch it and complete the track."
The player naturally grabs it with their fingers, snaps it into the gap, and watches the chain reaction fire.
Instant dopamine, zero motion sickness, 100% intuitive.
```
